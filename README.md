# 8-Bit Arcade Games

Two original retro pixel-art browser games. Each game is a single HTML file with no installs and no build step.

## Games

### Horizon Ace
A top-down flying shooter. Fly one complete mission from morning to sunset, over coastal islands, a river canyon and snowy peaks, then land at the harbour airfield.

- Enemy scouts, fighters and bombers, barrage balloons, storm clouds and hostile gunboats
- Moving ships at sea: cargo ships, passenger liners, navy patrol ships and fishing boats
- Synthesized arcade sound effects with a mute button
- Lives, fuel, power-ups and a saved high score

**Controls:** Arrow keys to fly, A, Space or J to fire, Shift or K to boost, Esc or P to pause, M for sound on/off, I for the How to Play card (also the i button).
On phones and tablets: on-screen pad to fly, FIRE and BOOST buttons, II to pause.

### Feather Flight
A side-scrolling bird flying game. Fly through blue skies with other birds, eat berries for energy and dodge hawks and storm clouds when the rain comes.

**Controls:** Arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space or Esc to pause.
On phones and tablets: on-screen pad to fly, II to pause.

## How to play

**On your computer:** download this repository (green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**), unzip it, and double-click `index.html` inside a game folder.

**Online:** if GitHub Pages is turned on for this repository, open the Pages link and pick a game.

All artwork is drawn in code and all sounds are generated in the browser. No third-party game assets are used.
